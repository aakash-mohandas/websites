GoMining Review: Passive Bitcoin Income Without Hardware

ABy Aakash · Updated Oct 2026

GoMining lets you own a share of real Bitcoin mining hardware through NFT miners — no rigs, no electricity bills, no noise. Here's what it's actually like to use.

What is GoMining?

GoMining is a platform that lets you participate in Bitcoin mining without purchasing, hosting, or maintaining physical ASIC hardware. You buy NFTs that represent real mining machines operating in professional data centres, and the platform handles power, cooling, maintenance, and pool management. You just collect BTC daily.

I've personally run a 1,000+ TH/s fleet on GoMining for over a year, and the appeal is simple: daily BTC income with zero operational overhead. The platform converts the underlying real-world mining economics (hashrate, efficiency, electricity cost, pool fees) into a simple NFT ownership model that anyone can buy into from a browser or mobile app.

How GoMining fees work

Cost componentHow it works
Miner NFT purchase priceOne-time cost, scales with hashrate (TH/s) of the miner
Daily maintenance feeCovers electricity + hosting, deducted from mined BTC daily
Marketplace trading feeSmall percentage fee when buying/selling miner NFTs
WithdrawalStandard on-chain network fee applies

The single most important number isn't hashrate — it's efficiency, measured in joules per terahash (J/TH). A lower J/TH number means the miner uses less electricity to produce the same hashrate, which directly reduces your daily maintenance fee relative to what you earn. Always compare efficiency, not just raw TH/s, before buying a miner NFT.

How to get started with GoMining

  1. Create an account using a referral link to unlock a welcome bonus.
  2. Browse the miner marketplace and compare hashrate, efficiency (J/TH), and price across available NFT miners.
  3. Purchase a miner using crypto (BTC, ETH, USDT) or a card, depending on supported payment methods in your region.
  4. Monitor daily payouts — BTC accrues to your in-app wallet daily based on your share of the mining pool's output, minus maintenance fees.
  5. Withdraw or reinvest — withdraw accumulated BTC to your own wallet, or use it to buy additional hashrate.

Who it's for

GoMining suits investors who are already bullish on Bitcoin and want a daily-accumulation mechanism layered on top of spot holdings. It's not a replacement for holding BTC directly — think of it as a dollar-cost-averaging engine that runs itself. It's not ideal for anyone seeking guaranteed returns or who isn't comfortable with platform/custodial risk.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoMining legit?

Yes, GoMining operates real ASIC mining hardware in professional data centres and pays out BTC daily based on your owned hashrate share. As with any custodial platform, you're trusting the company's operations, so treat it as platform risk rather than self-custodied mining.

How much does GoMining cost?

You purchase NFT miners representing a share of real hashrate, then pay ongoing maintenance fees that cover electricity and hosting. Lower-efficiency miners cost more in fees relative to their output.

Can I sell my GoMining miners?

Yes, miner NFTs are tradeable on GoMining's built-in marketplace, so you can exit your position by selling to another user rather than only withdrawing earned BTC.

What's a good efficiency (J/TH) to look for?

Lower is better — newer-generation miners typically offer meaningfully lower J/TH than older ones. Compare the efficiency rating across available NFT miners before buying, since it directly determines how much of your output gets eaten by maintenance fees.

Is GoMining better than just buying Bitcoin?

Not necessarily — GoMining layers a daily-accumulation mechanism on top of Bitcoin exposure, but it underperforms simply holding spot BTC in flat or declining markets due to maintenance fees. It suits people who want to combine mining-style accumulation with their existing BTC strategy, not replace it.
