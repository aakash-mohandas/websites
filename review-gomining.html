GoMining Review: Passive Bitcoin Income Without Hardware

ABy Aakash Mohandas · Updated Oct 2026

GoMining lets you own a share of real Bitcoin mining hardware through NFT miners — no rigs, no electricity bills, no noise. Here's what it's actually like to use.

What is GoMining?

GoMining is a platform that lets you participate in Bitcoin mining without purchasing, hosting, or maintaining physical ASIC hardware. You buy NFTs that represent real mining machines operating in professional data centres, and the platform handles power, cooling, maintenance, and pool management. You just collect BTC daily.

I've personally run a 1,000+ TH/s fleet on GoMining for over a year, and the appeal is simple: daily BTC income with zero operational overhead.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Daily BTC payouts, no minimums
  • No electricity costs or hardware setup
  • Miner NFTs are tradeable on the marketplace
  • VIP rewards and Miner Wars competitions
  • No technical knowledge required

Cons

  • Platform risk — it's a centralised custodian
  • Maintenance fees eat into returns at low efficiency
  • Underperforms spot BTC in flat/bear markets

Who it's for

GoMining suits investors who are already bullish on Bitcoin and want a daily-accumulation mechanism layered on top of spot holdings. It's not a replacement for holding BTC directly — think of it as a dollar-cost-averaging engine that runs itself.

💡 Efficiency (J/TH) matters more than raw hashrate. Lower efficiency miners cost more in maintenance fees over time.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoMining legit?

Yes, GoMining operates real ASIC mining hardware in professional data centres and pays out BTC daily based on your owned hashrate share. As with any custodial platform, you're trusting the company's operations, so treat it as platform risk rather than self-custodied mining.

How much does GoMining cost?

You purchase NFT miners representing a share of real hashrate, then pay ongoing maintenance fees that cover electricity and hosting. Lower-efficiency miners cost more in fees relative to their output.

Can I sell my GoMining miners?

Yes, miner NFTs are tradeable on GoMining's built-in marketplace, so you can exit your position by selling to another user rather than only withdrawing earned BTC.
