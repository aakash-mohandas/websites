Kraken Pro Review: One of Crypto's Most Trusted Exchanges

Kraken has been around since 2011 and has never suffered a major hack. Here's why it's one of my go-to exchanges for serious trading.

What is Kraken Pro?

Kraken Pro is the advanced trading interface for Kraken, one of the longest-running and most secure crypto exchanges in the world. It supports 200+ assets, advanced order types, and institutional-grade charting tools, all with competitive fees that scale down as your volume increases.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Industry-leading security track record since 2011
  • 200+ cryptocurrencies available
  • Advanced charting and order types for serious traders
  • Transparent, volume-based fee tiers

Cons

  • Interface has a learning curve for beginners
  • Not available in all jurisdictions
  • Customer support can be slow during high volatility

Who it's for

Kraken Pro suits traders who value security and a long operating track record over flashy features. If you're moving meaningful capital and want peace of mind, Kraken's history speaks for itself.

🔒 Kraken has never suffered a successful exchange-level hack in over a decade of operation — a rare claim in crypto.
