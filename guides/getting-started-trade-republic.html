How to Get Started with Trade Republic

ABy Aakash · Updated Oct 2026

Trade Republic makes commission-free ETF investing simple. Here's how to open an account and set up your first recurring savings plan.

Before you start

Trade Republic is available in several European markets. You'll need a government ID, your tax ID (where applicable), and a bank account in a supported country.

Step 1: Download the app and register

Install Trade Republic and sign up with your phone number and email. You'll be asked for your tax residency details as part of standard financial regulation.

Step 2: Verify your identity

Complete identity verification via a short video call or photo ID check, depending on your country. This usually takes just a few minutes.

Step 3: Fund your account

Transfer money from your linked bank account. Transfers typically settle within one business day; some instant transfer options may be available depending on your bank.

💡 Pro tip: Trade Republic charges a flat €1 fee per trade rather than a percentage — this makes it especially cost-effective for larger trade sizes.

Step 4: Set up a savings plan

Search for an ETF or stock, tap "Savings Plan," and choose an amount and frequency (weekly, biweekly, or monthly). Savings plans execute automatically and are a simple way to dollar-cost average without manual effort.

Step 5: Make a one-off trade

To buy or sell manually, search for the instrument, choose market or limit order, confirm quantity, and execute. Orders outside regular exchange hours route through partner execution venues.

Understanding deposit protection

Cash held with Trade Republic is protected under German deposit insurance up to €100,000, and securities are held in your name, segregated from the company's own assets — worth understanding before you commit larger sums.
