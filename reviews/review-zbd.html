ZBD Review: Earning Real Bitcoin by Playing Games

ABy Aakash · Updated Oct 2026

ZBD pays out real BTC over the Lightning Network for playing mobile games, completing surveys, and quests. It's free, fast, and surprisingly fun.

What is ZBD?

ZBD is a Lightning Network wallet and rewards app in one. You play casual mobile games, complete surveys or quests, and get paid in real satoshis instantly — no points system, no redemption thresholds. It integrates directly with a growing catalog of mobile games, meaning the sats land in your wallet the moment you hit a milestone, not after a delayed "redemption" process.

I use it casually alongside my other crypto holdings, mostly as a zero-cost way to stack a few extra sats during downtime. Because withdrawals happen over Lightning, they're near-instant and essentially feeless compared to on-chain Bitcoin transactions.

How to get started with ZBD

  1. Download the ZBD app using a referral link for any available sign-up bonus.
