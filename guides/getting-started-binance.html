How to Get Started with Binance

ABy Aakash · Updated Oct 2026

Binance has the widest range of coins and trading pairs of any major exchange. Here's how to set up your account properly and avoid common beginner mistakes.

Before you start

Have a government ID ready, plus a phone number and either a bank account or existing crypto to deposit. Binance's sheer size means verification queues and support response times can vary, so plan a little patience into your first setup.

Step 1: Create your account

Sign up with your email or phone number. Use a unique, strong password and avoid reusing credentials from other platforms.

Step 2: Complete identity verification

Submit a government-issued ID and a selfie for facial verification. Basic verification unlocks fiat deposits and higher withdrawal limits; it's usually processed within minutes to a few hours.

Step 3: Fund your account

You can deposit crypto directly from another wallet or exchange, or buy crypto with a card or bank transfer. Card purchases are fastest but carry higher fees than bank transfers.

Step 4: Secure your account

Enable two-factor authentication using an authenticator app rather than SMS, which is vulnerable to SIM-swap attacks. Consider also setting up anti-phishing codes, which Binance includes in all official emails to help you spot fakes.

💡 Pro tip: withdrawal address whitelisting adds a delay before new addresses can receive funds — turn it on, it's one of the best anti-theft protections available.

Step 5: Make your first trade

For simplicity, use the "Convert" feature to swap one asset for another at a quoted rate with no order book required. Once comfortable, the Spot trading view gives you limit orders and lower fees, especially if you hold BNB to pay fees at a discount.

Explore BNB fee discounts

Holding and using BNB to pay trading fees gives a meaningful discount compared to paying in your base currency — worth doing from day one if you plan to trade actively.
