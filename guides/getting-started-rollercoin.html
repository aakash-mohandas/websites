How to Get Started with RollerCoin

ABy Aakash · Updated Oct 2026

RollerCoin turns virtual crypto mining into a game — build a rig, play mini-games, and earn real crypto for free. Here's how to set it up.

Before you start

RollerCoin is free to play and doesn't require any purchase or deposit to begin earning. You only need an email address to create an account.

Step 1: Create a free account

Sign up with your email and choose a username. You'll land in your virtual "mining city" where your rig and games live.

Step 2: Build your starter rig

Use the in-game currency you start with to add your first virtual miners. More powerful miners increase your hashpower, which determines your share of daily crypto rewards — similar in concept to real mining pools, but entirely virtual under the hood.

Step 3: Play mini-games to boost power

RollerCoin's arcade-style mini-games let you earn extra hashpower and in-game currency just by playing — no purchase necessary. This is the main way free players grow their rig without spending money.

💡 Pro tip: daily login streaks and mini-game leaderboards offer some of the best free hashpower boosts — check in regularly rather than playing in one long session.

Step 4: Join a mining pool

Choose a mining pool to combine your hashpower with other players and increase your share of daily payouts in BTC, ETH, DOGE and other supported coins.

Step 5: Withdraw your earnings

Once you've accumulated enough balance, withdraw to an external wallet. Minimum withdrawal thresholds apply and vary by coin, so check current limits before cashing out small amounts.

Free vs. paid play

RollerCoin can be played entirely free, though optional paid upgrades accelerate rig growth. If you're just curious about crypto mining mechanics without any financial risk, the free tier alone is enough to get a feel for it.
