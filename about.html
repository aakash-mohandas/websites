About ParsedThoughts

I'm Aakash — a cloud infrastructure engineer who got curious about crypto exchanges, investing apps and passive Bitcoin mining, and started writing down what I learned. ParsedThoughts is the result.

Why trust these reviews?

Every platform reviewed on this site is one I have personally signed up for, funded with real money, and used over weeks or months — not a platform I read about and summarized from a press kit. Where I quote fees, security history or performance numbers, I verify them directly against the platform's own documentation at the time of writing.

I work full-time automating cloud infrastructure, which means I spend my days thinking about uptime, security and reliability — the same lens I apply when judging whether a crypto exchange or investing app is actually trustworthy, not just well-marketed.

How this site makes money

Some links on review pages are referral or affiliate links. If you sign up through one of these links, I may earn a commission or bonus at no extra cost to you. This never changes what I write — I turned down writing about platforms I didn't rate highly, and I disclose pros and cons honestly, including for platforms I use and recommend myself. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How reviews are written and updated

Each review is based on hands-on use of the platform, cross-checked against the provider's current fee schedules and terms. Reviews are revisited periodically (noted by the "Updated" date on each page) to catch fee changes, new features, or anything that changes my recommendation.

Get in touch

Spotted something outdated, or have a platform you'd like reviewed? Email me directly at hello@parsedthoughts.com.
