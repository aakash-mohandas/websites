Privacy Policy

ParsedThoughts does not run ads, trackers, or analytics scripts, and does not collect personal data directly. This page explains exactly what happens when you visit.

What we don't do

  • No cookies are set by this site
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts
  • No account creation, newsletters, or forms that collect personal data
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties

Hosting & server logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like virtually all web infrastructure, Cloudflare automatically processes standard technical data for every request — such as your IP address, browser type, and request timestamps — in order to serve pages and protect against abuse (e.g. DDoS mitigation). We do not have access to personally identify visitors from this data, and it is not used for marketing or profiling. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details on how they process this information.

Affiliate & referral links

Some links on this site (for example on our individual platform review pages) are affiliate or referral links. If you sign up through one of these links, we may earn a commission or bonus at no extra cost to you. This never affects the honesty of our reviews — we only recommend platforms we actually use.

External links

This site links out to third-party platforms (exchanges, brokers, apps). Once you leave ParsedThoughts, that platform's own privacy policy and terms apply — we have no control over how they handle your data.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store any personal data ourselves, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about data processed by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) or any linked third-party platform, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this site ever adds analytics, newsletters, or any feature that collects personal data, this page will be updated accordingly and a cookie/consent banner will be added where legally required.
