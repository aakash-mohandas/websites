How to Get Started with ZBD

ABy Aakash · Updated Oct 2026

ZBD lets you earn real Bitcoin (sats) by playing games and completing offers, paid out instantly over the Lightning Network. Here's how to get set up.

Before you start

ZBD is free to join and doesn't require any deposit. You just need an email or phone number to sign up — the built-in Lightning wallet handles everything else.

Step 1: Download the app and sign up

Install ZBD from the App Store or Google Play and register with your email or phone number. No KYC is required to start earning small amounts.

Step 2: Explore your built-in wallet

ZBD automatically creates a Lightning Network Bitcoin wallet for you — no separate wallet setup needed. This is what makes instant, near-zero-fee payouts possible.

Step 3: Earn your first sats

Browse the Game Center for titles that pay out in sats for playing, hitting milestones, or completing in-app achievements. The Offer Wall also pays sats for completing surveys, app trials, or sign-ups with partner services.

💡 Pro tip: payouts per game vary a lot — check a title's estimated earn rate before investing significant time in it.

Step 4: Withdraw or spend your sats

Once you've accumulated sats, withdraw them to an external Lightning wallet, or spend them directly within supported games and partner apps. Lightning withdrawals are typically near-instant with minimal fees.

Setting expectations

ZBD won't replace a salary — think of it as a way to earn genuine Bitcoin from time you'd spend gaming anyway, or as a low-effort introduction to how Lightning payments work in practice.
