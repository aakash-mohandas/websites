Revolut Review: Multi-Currency Banking Done Right

Revolut has 70 million+ users for a reason: fee-free international spending, interbank exchange rates, and crypto all in one app. Here's the full picture.

What is Revolut?

Revolut is a digital banking app offering multi-currency accounts, fee-free international spending, and in-app access to crypto, stocks and commodities. It's become a go-to for frequent travellers and anyone tired of hidden FX fees from traditional banks.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Multi-currency accounts and cards
  • Fee-free international spending at interbank rates
  • Crypto, stocks and commodities accessible in-app
  • Referral reward for both parties on sign-up

Cons

  • Free tier has withdrawal and exchange limits
  • Premium features require a paid plan
  • Customer support is primarily in-app chat

Who it's for

Revolut is excellent for frequent travellers, remote workers paid in multiple currencies, or anyone who wants a single app for spending, saving, and light investing abroad.

💳 The interbank exchange rate on currency conversion alone can save meaningful money versus a traditional bank card.
