GoMining Card Explained: Spend Your Crypto Anywhere Visa Works

ABy Aakash · Updated Oct 2026

Mining BTC daily is great, but at some point you want to actually spend it. The GoMining Card lets you do that directly from your mining earnings — here's how it works and who can get one.

What is the GoMining Card?

The GoMining Card is a virtual (and eventually physical) Visa debit card linked directly to your GoMining crypto wallet balance. When you pay with it, whichever crypto asset you've selected gets converted to the purchase currency — EUR, USD, or whatever you're spending in — instantly, at the moment of the transaction. There's no separate top-up step: the card draws straight from your existing GoMining wallet balance.

In practice, this means the BTC your miners earn daily doesn't have to sit idle or get manually withdrawn to an exchange before you can use it — you can spend it like a normal debit card anywhere Visa is accepted.

Which crypto assets can you spend?

You choose which asset the card draws from for payments. Supported options are:

  • GOMINING — the platform's native token
  • BTC — useful if you want to spend straight from your mining payouts
  • USDT
  • USDC

Conversion happens per-transaction at the live exchange rate, so you're not pre-converting a balance — each purchase settles against whatever rate applies at that moment.

What you get with the card

  • Issued instantly once your application is approved — no waiting for a physical card to arrive before you can use it
  • Ready for online payments immediately after issuance
  • Works with Apple Pay and Google Wallet for contactless NFC payments
  • Secured with a PIN plus a static 3DS password for online transactions

Eligibility and KYC requirements

This is the part most people underestimate: GoMining Cards aren't available to everyone automatically. You need to complete both KYC Basic and KYC Advanced verification before you can issue or use a card — this is stricter than the identity verification needed just to mine on the platform.

Requirements vary slightly depending on where you live:

🌍 Europe (EEA+ and listed European jurisdictions): you'll need a valid national passport or national ID card, plus proof of address no older than 3 months. A driver's license or residence permit alone isn't accepted as primary ID — though non-European citizens living in Europe may additionally need a residence permit to confirm residency.
🌐 Rest of the world: a valid national passport or national ID (where supported), plus proof of address from a non-prohibited jurisdiction, no older than 3 months. Your ID and proof of address don't need to be from the same country.

GoMining maintains a list of prohibited jurisdictions where card issuance isn't allowed at all, in line with applicable sanctions and regulatory requirements — and that list can change over time, so it's worth checking the official documentation if you're unsure whether your country qualifies.

One notable restriction: passport holders from Belarus and Russia can only apply if they hold a valid residence permit issued by an EEA+ country.

Balance requirements and card limits

To issue your first card, you need at least $100 in your GoMining wallet (counted across all your assets combined, not just one). Once that first card exists, additional cards don't require any minimum balance. You can have up to 3 active virtual cards at a time, and issuing a card is free — GoMining doesn't charge for creating any of them.

Is it worth getting?

If you're already mining on GoMining and comfortable completing full KYC verification, the card is a genuinely convenient way to close the loop between earning BTC and actually spending it, without routing through a separate exchange withdrawal first. If you're not planning to go through Advanced KYC, or you're in a prohibited jurisdiction, it's simply not available to you — in which case, normal wallet withdrawals to an exchange remain your path to spending your mined BTC.

💡 The KYC Advanced requirement is the main gatekeeper here. If you only completed KYC Basic when you started mining, budget time to complete the additional verification step before you can apply for a card.
