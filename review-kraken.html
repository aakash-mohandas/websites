Frequently asked questions

Is Kraken Pro safe to use?

Yes. Kraken has operated since 2011 and has never suffered a successful exchange-level hack, which is a rare track record in the crypto industry. It also holds proof-of-reserves audits and offers advanced account security like global settings lock.

What are Kraken Pro's trading fees?

Kraken Pro uses a volume-based maker/taker fee schedule, starting around 0.25%/0.40% for low-volume traders and scaling down significantly as your 30-day trading volume increases.

Is Kraken Pro good for beginners?

Kraken Pro's advanced interface has a learning curve, so complete beginners may prefer Kraken's simpler standard interface first. Once comfortable, Kraken Pro offers more control over order types and fees.
