Trade Republic Review: Commission-Free Stocks, ETFs & Savings

Trade Republic combines commission-free investing with a high-yield savings account in one elegant app. Here's my experience using it across Europe.

What is Trade Republic?

Trade Republic is a broker and bank hybrid that lets you invest in stocks, ETFs and crypto commission-free, while also offering a competitive high-yield savings account on uninvested cash. It's widely available across Europe, including Ireland.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Commission-free stocks and ETFs
  • High-yield savings account on idle cash
  • Welcome bonus on account creation
  • Clean, simple mobile-first interface

Cons

  • Limited advanced charting compared to pro platforms
  • Only available in select European markets
  • Fractional share availability varies by asset

Who it's for

Trade Republic is ideal for long-term investors in Europe who want a no-fuss way to build an ETF portfolio and earn yield on cash, without paying per-trade commissions.

📈 The savings account rate is especially useful for parking cash between investment decisions.
